# Outreach kit: Trade Lead Check

Only post where the group admin allows it. No cold SMS, WhatsApp or email to sole traders (PECR). Ask first, or hand over a flyer.

## Facebook group post (ask the admin first)
Plumbers, sparkies, builders: are Checkatrade, Bark or MyBuilder paying for themselves?
I built a free 30-second check. You type in last month's spend, enquiries and jobs won, and it tells you cost per job and whether to keep, renegotiate or cancel. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
[link]
If it tells you something useful (or is wrong), tell me. I'm a London developer testing it with trades.

## Counter chat (6:30 to 8am, trade counters)
"Quick one, do you pay for Checkatrade, Bark or similar? I made a free calculator that shows what each job really costs you. Mind if I show you on your phone?"
Ask: "What did you pay last month, and how many jobs did it give you?" Write the answer down. Note whether they raised any pain unprompted.

## Reply scripts
- Asked how it works: "Seven numbers from last month in, a verdict out. It runs in your browser and I never see your numbers."
- Verdict says cancel/renegotiate: "Want me to go through your last 3 months of leads and invoices? £99, or free if I find nothing. Send them over and I'll reply within 48 hours."
- Verdict says answer faster: "Missed enquiries were the leak. I can draft quick-reply texts you send from your own number."
- Asks about old customers: "I can clean your old invoice list and draft service-reminder texts you send yourself. Free sample for 20 customers."

## 20-second screen recording
1. Open the page on a phone. 2. Tap "Try example numbers". 3. Pause 3 seconds on the stamp. 4. Tap "Print or save as PDF". Caption: "Illustrative example numbers."

## Tracking (tally per week)
Completions (page counts), counter conversations, pain raised unprompted, audit requests, old-customer requests, £ already spent on platforms.
Kill rule: under 30 completions and under 3 audit requests after 3 weeks posting. 3+ requests: build the reactivation kit.
