How to tell if Checkatrade, Bark, MyBuilder or Rated People is worth it

Divide what you paid by the jobs you won. If you paid £240 and won 4 jobs, each job cost you £60. Then compare that with the profit a job earns you before the fee. If the fee is bigger, the platform is losing you money.

What counts as a good cost per job?

It depends on your trade and margin. Cost per job should sit well below the profit that job earns you. Also check how many enquiries you actually answered: replies that come too late rarely turn into work.

Can I cancel a platform contract early?

Check your agreement before you act. Many memberships run for 12 months, and some tradespeople say the minimum term wasn't clear when they signed up. Read the cancellation terms and notice period first, and ask the platform for a lower price or a shorter term before you renew.
